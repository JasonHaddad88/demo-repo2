05 · Straight talk

What a trillion-dollar month actually requires.

The arithmetic

  • $1T/month is $12T/year, about 11% of everything humanity produces, and roughly 18× the revenue of the largest company on Earth.
  • Even if every one of the ~8.2 billion people alive paid you, each would have to spend about $122 a month.
  • No company has ever been close. The engine above tells you exactly how far off any plan is.

What actually compounds

  • A problem painful enough that people pay to fix it this week.
  • Unit economics that improve with scale, so each customer costs less and earns more.
  • Distribution you own: brand, network effects, or a channel others can't copy.
  • Years of iteration. A website is just the storefront, and the business behind it is what earns money.
